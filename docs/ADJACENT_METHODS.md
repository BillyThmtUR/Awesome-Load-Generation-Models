# Adjacent generative approaches

These works provide methodological comparison, but are **not** GAN or DDPM load generators and should not be mislabelled.

| Year | Paper | Method | URL |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 2026 | SmartMeterFM: Unifying Smart Meter Data Generative Tasks Using Flow Matching Models | Conditional flow matching, monthly profiles at 15 min | https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.21706 |
| 2026 | Month-long conditioned synthesis of half-hourly household gas and electricity data with an autoregressive transformer (Synthetic-SERL) | Autoregressive transformer, not diffusion Transformer | https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10228107/ |
| 2025 | A flow-based model for conditional and probabilistic electricity consumption profile generation | Normalizing flow | https://doi.org/10.1016/j.egyai.2025.100586 |
| 2023 | Generating realistic load profiles in smart grids: An approach based on nonlinear independent component estimation (NICE) and convolutional layers | Invertible flow | https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apenergy.2023.121902 |
| 2024 | A Survey on Diffusion Models for Time Series and Spatio-Temporal Data | General survey; no direct claim of originality in electrical load | https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.18886 |
